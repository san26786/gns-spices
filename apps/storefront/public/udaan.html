First, the plain truth

UDAAN is not an MLM.
UDAAN is not a traditional distribution channel either.

UDAAN is a new-generation, community-powered supply-chain model designed to make everyday commerce more rewarding. We are an FMCG distribution and marketing ecosystem. Here is the difference — plainly.

In an MLM
  • ✗ You pay to join
  • ✗ You earn by recruiting people
  • ✗ You sell overpriced products nobody needs
In GNS
  • ✓ Joining is 100% free
  • ✓ You earn ₹0 for recruiting anyone
  • ✓ You deal in daily kitchen essentials — spices, papads, pickles, ₹10 sachets — every household cooks with them every day

UDYAM is simply a modern B2B distribution channel for warehouse stockists. UDAAN is a community-led demand engine that replaces expensive TV commercials with local word-of-mouth earnings.

Legally, ethically, structurally

The four pillars that prove GNS is pure FMCG commerce.

Four specific structural rules. Break any one and this becomes an MLM. All four holding is why it isn't.

01

Zero recruitment rewards

Strict legal & ethical

You earn ₹0 for signing up 10, 100 or 1,000 people. Commissions flow only when a physical product reaches a real customer or Kirana shop.

02

Everyday FMCG price integrity

Fair MRP · no inflation

Genuine kitchen essentials at fair prices. Sales driven by organic consumer pull — not inflated MLM-style margins or earnings promises.

03

Decoupled — no stock-loading

Supply ≠ Demand

UDAAN advocates carry ZERO inventory — never buy, store or ship stock. UDYAM District Distributors hold 100% of stock in audited warehouses.

04

Non-pipelining governance

Role-based · not top-heavy

Missing intermediate tiers do not become middleman windfalls. Under the 70:30 model, those funds revert to the U6 / A5 Performance Growth Pools.
